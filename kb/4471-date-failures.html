Shared parser: 379 reports

The common parser treats every numeric date as MM/DD/YYYY. A tenant that writes DD/MM/YYYY therefore has its day and month exchanged whenever both values can be read as months. Intake labels are guesses; tenant configuration determines the cause.

Five reports were read by the desk as a one-day move. Their tenant locale and booking form place them in this parser cause, so they remain counted here.

booking-v3 hint: 33 reports

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field for day-first tenants. People follow that instruction, but the form interprets their input with the tenant's day-first order. This is a form-contract defect, separate from the shared parser defect.

Counting method

The export repeats two report IDs because ticket-system pages overlap. Counts use one record per report ID, then classify a report from the tenant directory: a day-first tenant's form report through booking-v3 belongs to the hint cause; every other report from a day-first tenant belongs to the parser cause.
